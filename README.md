# MUX4QCA

Nekonvencionalne platforme in metode procesiranja: Optimalnost Optimalnega 4-vhodnega Multiplekserja

## Implementirana vezja

### Iz literature

- [opti4mux.qca](./opti4mux.qca) je iz [Optimum multiplexer design in quantum-dot cellular automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00360)
- [mux2_1.qca](./mux2_1.qca) je iz [Optimum multiplexer design in quantum-dot cellular automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00360)
- [mux2_2.qca](./mux2_2.qca) je iz [Optimum multiplexer design in quantum-dot cellular automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00360)
- [re4mux.qca](./re4mux.qca) je iz [Towards sustainable QCA computing: Robust and energy-efficient 2:1 multiplexer and D-latch designs](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2210537926001496)
- [optinmux4.qca](./optinmux4.qca) je iz [An optimal design of QCA based 2n:1/1:2n multiplexer/demultiplexer and its efficient digital logic realization](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0141933117303204)
- [mux2_ram.qca](./mux2_ram.qca) je iz [Optimal design of RAM cell using novel 2:1 multiplexer in QCA technology](https://www.emerald.com/cw/article-abstract/46/2/147/32677/Optimal-design-of-RAM-cell-using-novel-2-1?redirectedFrom=fulltext)

### Lastni dizajni

- [opti4mux_moy.qca](./opti4mux_moy.qca) je osnovan na podlagi [Optimum multiplexer design in quantum-dot cellular automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00360) vendar uporablja [An optimal design of QCA based 2n:1/1:2n multiplexer/demultiplexer and its efficient digital logic realization](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0141933117303204) za zadnji MUX 2.
